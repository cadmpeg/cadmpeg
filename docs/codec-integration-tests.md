# Codec integration-test partitions

The integration suites use generated byte images only. They do not read the private format corpus at test time, and no corpus payload is embedded, transformed, or copied into the repository. Private files are used only to identify container variants and combinations of format features that a synthesized image must represent.

Each suite contains seven tests. A test is a partition of behavior rather than a single decoder primitive: it enters through detection, inspection, `Codec::decode`, preserved-source writing, or source-less writing and checks the resulting neutral IR, native namespace, source accounting, and validation where the format supports those stages.

## Shared pipeline

The input pipeline is format detection, bounded container framing, logical stream classification, native record parsing, native-reference resolution, neutral geometry/topology projection, design/product/presentation projection, loss and byte-accounting finalization, and IR validation. Preserved-source writers add semantic diffing and native record patching. Source-less writers add target validation and native serialization.

Detection and outer-container enumeration are the low-complexity stages. The high-complexity stages are record-boundary recovery, reference resolution across independently framed streams, unit and placement application exactly once, loop/radial topology reconstruction, procedural and freeform carrier binding, semantic ownership, and atomic refusal when a writer cannot represent an edit.

The baseline workspace test run covered the codec source lines as follows: CATIA 90.64%, Creo 87.04%, F3D 77.89%, FreeCAD 77.47%, IGES 85.53%, NX 90.45%, Rhino 76.42%, SLDPRT 88.37%, and STEP 89.63%. Existing focused tests already covered most individual record parsers. The new partitions target cross-stage combinations, format variants, low-coverage projection modules, writer paths, and recovery behavior.

## CATIA CATPart

Behavior universe: compound-document framing; standard nested V5, FBB-only, zero-entity, E5, A8/B5 float-packed, and container-only variants; standard and reconstructed topology; analytic and NURBS carriers; parametric supports; object/design data; source accounting.

The prior gaps were interaction between variant selection and semantic transfer, standard radial topology as a complete graph, FBB carrier-only behavior, zero-entity support binding, E5 precedence, external A8 control grids, and variant-specific container-only retention.

The seven partitions are: standard detection/inspection/decode alignment; standard radial graph; FBB-only transfer; zero-entity support; coherent E5 precedence; float-packed A8 grid; all-variant container-only retention.

## Creo PRT

Behavior universe: PSB framing and ND section layouts; visible-geometry tables; analytic/freeform carriers; intersection topology; datum and model-status history; FeatDefs sketches, dimensions, relations, solver tables, and feature semantics; preview and opaque retention.

The prior gaps were cross-section roles in inspection, complete prototype placement, full closed topology construction, datum/history correlation, mixed sketch projection, solver-table retention through neutral projection, and multi-domain container-only behavior.

The seven partitions are: PSB roles; analytic prototype placement; closed plane-intersection solid; datum/history merge; mixed sketch entities and native constraints; solver incidences and relation triples; geometry/design/thumbnail container-only retention.

## Autodesk Fusion F3D/F3Z

Behavior universe: ZIP and nested ZIP framing; ASM BinaryFile record streams and history partitions; analytic, NURBS, pcurve, and procedural geometry; Protein properties; design streams, sketches, materials, configurations, and history; external F3Z occurrence graphs; retained and source-less writing.

The prior gaps were concentrated in validation, profile selection, face resolution, and design feature/material/history projection. Existing tests were deep within individual ASM record families but did not compose the complete archive domains into seven stable contracts.

The seven partitions are: F3D container and B-rep pipeline; geometry-family matrix; Protein/design/history/configuration correlation; preserved semantic geometry edits; source-less writer; recursive F3Z occurrence merge and cycle recovery; container-only native retention.

## FreeCAD FCStd

Behavior universe: stored, deflated, streamed, data-descriptor, and ZIP64 framing; `Document.xml` persistence graph; typed and dynamic properties; text and binary OpenCascade B-rep; element maps; application payloads; product, joint, GUI, drawing, and annotation domains; checked mutation and writing.

The prior gaps were concentrated in mutation, OpenCascade carrier parsing, element-map binding, source-less application graph writing, and cross-domain projection. The XML declaration graph and ZIP ledger are straightforward; referenced side entries and persisted placements make semantic transfer difficult.

The seven partitions are: ZIP layout matrix; typed graph build/mutate/write; exact geometry/topology/pcurve/mesh/placement composition; sketch/conic/NURBS/constraint composition; feature-family composition; product/application/GUI/drawing composition; atomic refusal and container-only retention.

## SolidWorks SLDPRT

Behavior universe: native block, cache, directory, and compound-document framing; compressed Parasolid partition and delta streams; analytic and NURBS geometry; topology ownership and seam derivation; configurations; resolved feature history, sketches, equations, materials, display lists, and PMI; retained and source-less semantic writing.

The prior gaps were concentrated in writer transforms, PMI, native ownership, and Parasolid recovery. Partition/delta identity, schema-specific record families, derived pcurves, and feature-input correlation are the difficult stages.

The seven partitions are: compound structures and metadata; Parasolid topology/geometry families; partition/delta/configuration merging; feature/sketch/constraint/dimension correlation; material/tessellation/PMI presentation; retained semantic regeneration; source-less fixed-point and refusal behavior.

## Siemens NX PRT

Behavior universe: SPLMSSTR framing; compressed Parasolid partition and delta streams; analytic, NURBS, procedural, offset, blend, intersection, and pcurve geometry; Display JT scene and mesh streams; indexed object-model records and feature history; attributes, arrangements, external references, and opaque assets.

The prior gaps were concentrated in Display JT, JT topology, and their correlation with native streams. Object-model input stores and document streams were well parsed individually but lacked broad integration contracts.

The seven partitions are: SPLMSSTR/Parasolid alignment; analytic topology matrix; freeform/procedural geometry matrix; delta replacement with topology preservation; Display JT stream matrix; composed object-model history; arrangements/attributes/external/opaque document data.

## Rhino 3DM

Behavior universe: OpenNURBS archive bands and width rules; table/chunk/CRC framing; settings, definitions, history, identity and attributes; curves, surfaces, B-rep, mesh, SubD, extrusion, instances, morph/cage/hatch; views, details, annotations, dimensions, and presentation; source-less writing.

The prior gaps were concentrated in views, document data, presentation, annotations, history, morph, and settings. Bounded anonymous chunks, archive-version gates, object-level transactionality, and instance expansion are the difficult stages.

The seven partitions are: archive-band and container-only matrix; curve and point-cloud composition; mesh/SubD/extrusion/B-rep composition; nested instance expansion; definitions/history/identity/settings composition; multi-version source-less writer; malformed-record atomic recovery.

## IGES 5.3

Behavior universe: Fixed ASCII cards, line endings, global delimiters and units, directory/parameter ownership, transforms; analytic/conic/spline/offset curves; analytic/NURBS/procedural/trimmed surfaces; explicit B-rep and seams; CSG, product, subfigure, group and external-reference structure; drawings, annotations, presentation, properties, and associativity.

The prior gaps were concentrated in entity evaluation, analytic surface and offset resolution, conics/splines, global data, and drawing projection. Fixed-width envelope parsing is straightforward; pointer graphs, transforms, trimming carriers, cyclic structures, and entity-form-dependent semantics are difficult.

The seven partitions are: envelope/global/unit/transform pipeline; curve-family matrix; surface-family matrix; topology/seam/void matrix; structure/product matrix; drawing/annotation matrix; property/attribute/associativity matrix.

## STEP Part 21

Behavior universe: Part 21 editions 2 and 3, header/data/anchor/reference/signature sections, strings and binary literals, opaque records and external dependencies; analytic, NURBS, procedural and trimmed geometry; B-rep, wire and tessellation topology; products and occurrences; styles, visibility, layers and PMI; AP203/AP214/AP242 writing and strict refusal.

The prior gaps were concentrated in string edge cases, geometry projection, and their interaction with writer policy. Lexing and flat entity framing are straightforward; complex entities, recursive references, context units, occurrence placements, schema edition differences, PMI graphs, and representation selection are difficult.

The seven partitions are: Part 21 envelope and retained data; geometry and units; topology and placements; product/assembly graphs; tessellation/presentation; semantic and presentation PMI; cross-schema writer and strict refusal.
